I am an independent researcher working on diffusion sampling and generative models for images and audio. In Dual-Solver, published at ICLR 2026, I studied a generalized ODE solver through parameterization of prediction types. In RBF-Solver, I explored multistep sampling with radial basis function interpolation. I also lead logp(x) at MODULABS, where we study the core principles of generative models.

My earlier research spans music and speech. At GAUDIOLAB, I worked on GTS for music–lyrics synchronization, and at VIVESTUDIOS, on VSpeech, an integrated TTS and voice conversion model. Earlier, I developed source separation and FOLLE sound generation models at GAUDIOLAB, and singing assessment and personalized TTS at SM Entertainment. Related papers include HCLAS-X for lyrics alignment, KimchiSpeech for speech synthesis, and GSEP for source separation. I am also a co-inventor on patents concerning speech generation, voice extraction and audio signal processing.

I studied Music Composition with a double major in Computer Science at Seoul National University. My graduation projects, the interactive music work WOBBLE and the ear-training application Earoid, brought together music and software.
